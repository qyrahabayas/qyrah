Care Guide

Caring for your Qyrah

WashingDry clean recommended. For lighter fabrics, hand wash cold with a gentle detergent; never wring.
DryingLay flat or hang on a padded hanger away from direct sunlight to protect the colour.
PressingSteam or press on low heat from the reverse, with a cloth over embroidery and beading.
StoringStore on a wide hanger in a breathable garment bag. Fold embellished pieces with tissue.

She Envisions.

Qyrah · The tagline

Three words. One full sentence. A period that does not ask for permission.

She

Specific. Not every woman. Her. The woman who holds her haya with pride and still wants to look extraordinary doing it.

“She” is specific and intentional. This brand is not for everyone. It is built for her. The woman with haya. The woman with ambition. The woman who does not see these as contradictions.

Envisions

Active. Forward-looking. She is not waiting. She sees the woman she is becoming and she is already dressing for her.

“Envisions” is not a passive word. It is the act of seeing what does not yet exist and choosing to move toward it anyway. She envisions the woman she is becoming. She envisions a life where her values and her style do not compete.

.

The full stop is part of the tagline. It signals certainty, not aspiration, not a promise still to be kept. It is already true.

The full stop is deliberate. “She Envisions.” is a complete thought. A declaration. Not a question, not a suggestion. She does. Period.

She Envisions.

“If you are a woman who wants to hold onto her haya and her modesty while still expressing her style, elegance, and individuality. Qyrah is for you.”

Sabeeha Abbas, Founder
